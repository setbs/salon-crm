# Version 1.0 System Design

This document defines mandatory version 1.0 modules. It is planning only and does not describe implemented production code.

## Version 1.0 Modules

Core modules:

* Booking
* Authentication and roles
* Calendar
* Clients
* Services
* Service categories
* Employees
* Portfolio
* Inventory
* Sales
* Payments
* Reviews
* Analytics
* Notifications
* Reports
* Settings

Future-only modules:

* SMS notifications
* Mobile application
* Loyalty program
* Gift cards
* Multi-salon support
* Public product checkout
* Service product/material consumption automation

## Analytics Module

Analytics is an admin-only read model over operational data.

Revenue analytics:

* Daily revenue
* Weekly revenue
* Monthly revenue
* Yearly revenue

Service analytics:

* Most popular services
* Service usage count
* Revenue per service
* Average order value per service

Service catalog design:

* Services belong to admin-managed categories.
* Admin can create, edit, enable, and disable service categories.
* Booking and public service pages should display services grouped by category.
* Service categories are part of v1 database and API planning.

Product analytics:

* Best-selling products
* Most profitable products
* Product stock overview
* Low stock reports

Client analytics:

* Total clients
* New clients per month
* Returning clients
* Top clients by spending
* Average client spending

Employee analytics:

* Revenue generated by employee
* Total appointments
* Total clients served
* Average review rating

Design notes:

* Compute v1 analytics from normalized tables first.
* Use date range filters consistently.
* Prepare employee metrics for multi-employee salons.
* Do not add multi-salon dimensions in v1.

## Notifications Module

Email notifications are mandatory in v1.

Required email notifications:

* Appointment reminder 24 hours before appointment
* Appointment reminder 2 hours before appointment
* Post-appointment thank you message
* Post-appointment review request

Design notes:

* Notification scheduling should be handled by a background job/worker layer.
* Notification sending must be logged in `notification_logs`.
* Use a channel abstraction with `email` as the only v1 channel.
* Keep SMS as future-only architecture, not implementation.

## Reports Module

Reports are admin-only CSV exports.

Required CSV exports:

* Clients export
* Appointments export
* Product sales export
* Products export
* Revenue reports export

Design notes:

* Generate CSV files server-side.
* Date range filters are required where relevant.
* Consider `report_export_logs` for auditability.
* Reports are separate from Analytics: Analytics is interactive metrics, Reports is export workflow.
